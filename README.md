LUMEN DRIFT is a neon deep-space arcade survival game drawn entirely with Canvas 2D, with all sound synthesized in WebAudio. You pilot a small glowing ship through waves of geometric drones (triangles, squares and pentagons, each with its own movement) that drift in from the edges and home in on you.

**How to play**

- Move with WASD or the arrow keys (click-drag also steers) and aim with the mouse.
- The ship auto-fires; hold click or Space for rapid fire.
- Destroyed drones drop star cores. Collect them to build a combo multiplier and charge your shield.
- You have 3 hull lives, with brief invulnerability after a hit.
- Every 5 waves an elite drone with much more health appears.
- P or Esc pauses; R restarts after game over (click works too).

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
